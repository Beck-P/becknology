**Status: agreed. Built: crowds (js/crowd.js), small events (js/events.js), races and the regatta (js/race.js), the party (js/party.js). Now building, in parallel sessions: the planets (see *Building it* at the end). Enigma parked.** Beck: "make our Becknology world a lot denser and more alive… really
big swings… make them each super unique and cohesive", then "each one should feel large but connected and unique."
Each item is marked **agreed** (Beck picked it), **proposed** (offered, waiting on Beck), **parked** (a note for

## Across every world
### Crowds — agreed ("need lots of people")
- **Lots of different-looking people**: 8–10 base bodies (tall, short, broad, elderly, child) in a few outfit shapes
  (coat, dress, overalls, jacket, robe), recoloured from each planet's wardrobe by key colours the way js/look.js does
  the pilot, plus small extras (hats, umbrellas, bags, baskets, a dog on a lead). No clones walking in step.
- **They go somewhere**: out of a door, to a stall, a queue at a counter, a bench for a while, in another door, off the
  edge of the map. Empty chairs indoors fill by the hour.
- **By the clock and the weather**: Arcadia's 8pm–1am ET rush packed, Lumar's square half empty in the rain (umbrellas
  up, people hurrying), the tavern full after dark.
- **Groups**: couples, a parent and child, three sailors, a tour group behind a guide.
- **They notice you**: step aside, a glance, a wave from someone you've helped, kids petting your dog.
- **The same for everyone**: worked out from the clock and the place (as the sloop traffic is), so two pilots see the
  same people without sending anything.
- **Not**: blocking a door, counter or path (they part round you); in places meant to be lonely (Midnight Isle, the
  Last Ridge, the Singularity, Enigma); a full conversation each (E gets a one-liner, now and then a small favour);
  slowing phones (a cap, lower on small screens).

### Small events — agreed
A few minutes each, seeded by the clock so everyone online gets the same one, noticed as you would in life (a crowd
gathering, a sound, people pointing); some leave something behind (a short sale, a coin, a choice). Lumar: a trader in
and a sale on the quay, the catch auctioned, a fiddler, a fight out of the tavern, a wedding, toy boats in the
fountain. Arcadia: a police chase overhead, NULL hijacking every screen, a celebrity on the Royale's carpet, a drone
dropping its parcel, a brownout block by block. Verdance: a hen loose, the mail coach, a balloon, kites, a rainbow.
Hoarfell: avalanche blasting, the ski patrol's sled, a sleigh, a snowball fight, the aurora. Sylvara: a lantern
procession, the spirit stag, Thaelin playing. Enigma (before it wakes): lights flickering down the ring, a door
opening by itself, a voice on the comms.

### Races against each other — agreed
Race anyone who's there with you (Beck: "I want to be able to race Alyssa"), not just a partner or a party: sailing (a course round buoys and islands, both sloops on the start
line), skiing (the same run side by side, not a ghost) and the climbing routes. Today you can only race Liv, Ysolde
and your own ghost.

### Playing together: a party — agreed (Beck's idea)
Invite someone to play together; from then on travel waits for both of you. Whoever sets off (the jump, the shuttle,
the sloop, a mine floor, a game) sends a boarding call; the other gets *Beck is casting off — come?* and the departure
waits with a ready check (*Waiting for Alyssa…*), or goes without them if you choose. Proposed details: on a jump the
two Wayfarers go together (side by side in the tunnel, parked side by side in orbit, each other's galleon out of the
window); a party member can come aboard the other's Wayfarer (private today); it pulls in what exists — sailing
together, following down the mine, *Go to*, playing games together.

## Arcadia — agreed: up, down and out
"Ritzier as you go up, poorer as you go down"; Coruscant, but darker and neon. **Every level its own place, large but
connected, with room to go side to side as well as up and down** — not a lift shaft with rooms on it. Each level has
its own food and drink, its own people, light, sound and weather.

**The big places bigger and deeper than the District street** (Beck: "a little bit bigger and deeper… maybe even go in on the levels,
it's not all just the outside"): the street is 60 × 16; the main hubs are more like 80–120 across and 40–60 deep (not every place: smaller ones can stay street-sized), and
the levels have insides as well as outsides — atriums many floors high, malls and arcades inside the towers, corridors
and stairwells between open terraces. **Really good views** the higher you go. **The bottom is dangerous — agreed, with fighting**: gangs and their turf in
the Undercity, scavenger drones and worse in the Sump, tougher the lower you go.

**Nightlife by level, the vibe changing as you go up**: the Sump an illegal rave in a drowned station; the Undercity
dive bars, an industrial club, the fight pit's bar; the District Club Void and the noodle bars; the Mid-Levels rooftop
cocktail lounges and a jazz bar; the Spires a champagne bar, a sky lounge and a private members' club.

Proposed layout: a grid of places, levels by districts, joined side to side by walking and up and down at a few
fixed cores (lift towers, the Loop's stations, stairs), each main place 40–80 tiles across like the District street:

| Level | West | Middle | East |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Spires** (above the smog; stars) | sky gardens and a tea salon | Halcyon's crown plaza | the opera, the sky docks, a champagne bar |
| **The Mid-Levels** | the Skywalk | Skyport 7 | a Loop station, terrace restaurants, a rooftop bar |
| **The District** (now) | the old quarter: lanterns, ramen, a rooftop shrine | the neon street | the spaceport, freighters berthed |
| **The Undercity** | the market warrens: a hawker hall, dive bars, noodle shops | the fight pit, scrapyards | the Undercity station, back-alley chrome |
| **The Sump** | the canals, a noodle boat | the drowned shrine | the rave in the old station |

- **Light by level**: the Spires white, gold and pastel under real stars; the Mid-Levels glass and cyan; the District
  pink and cyan in the rain; the Undercity sodium orange and green tubes in steam, rain dripping through grates; the
  Sump teal glow on black water.
- **Every place shows the others**: across the gap to the next tower (side to side), down onto the smog with tower tops
  through it from the Spires, up at the District's underside from below; level numbers on the signs.
- **Getting around**: the Loop, a sky train circling the Mid-Levels through the towers (a carriage you sit in while
  the skyline streams by; it passes over the District street every few minutes); express lifts with the floors rolling
  past; air cabs between pads. No funicular (Beck: the sky train, express lifts "and stuff like that").
- **Implants — agreed**, tiered by level: back-alley chrome below (cheap, now and then it glitches), clinic-grade on the
  District, designer up top.
- The Map tab shows Arcadia as a side-on cutaway.
- Inspirations: Coruscant, Midgar, Kowloon Walled City, Hong Kong, Chongqing, Blade Runner, Ghost in the Shell,
  Altered Carbon.
- **Out**: the heist, rooftop running and courier jobs.

## Lumar — agreed: harbours that work, a cliff town, the spore bloom; every sea its own
- **Harbours that work — agreed**: the fleet out at dawn and home at dusk, a fish auction, ships unloading, a crane,
  sailmakers and ropewalks at work.
- **A cliff town — agreed.** **The spore bloom — agreed**: a night or two a month the whole sea lights up.
- **Each sea's people and towns more their own — agreed** ("the Emerald, Sapphire and Rose seas are your different
  places… then the Midnight Sea… the sorceress… the Crimson with the dangerous weather and the dragon's lair"), and
  **Crownhaven a lot bigger and cooler — agreed**. Proposed:
  - **Emerald, the Crown's sea**: Crownhaven as a capital of districts — the walled old town, a canal quarter of
    merchant houses, Guild Row (sailmakers, chartmakers, instrument makers), the naval academy and observatory on the
    hill, the Arsenal (the Royal Yard grown), a bridge over the harbour mouth with shops and houses on it, a funicular
    up to the palace, a theatre, the stilt slums under the quays.
  - **Sapphire, the merchants' sea**: glassblowers and the floating market; the cliff town here — white houses and
    blue domes round a drowned caldera, lanterns zigzagging down to the water.
  - **Rose, the crystal sea**: makers and mystics — crystal terraces, a bathhouse town on the hot springs, the silk
    groves, tea houses.
  - **Crimson, the dangerous sea**: a pirate port in a wreck-strewn cove, the volcano, the dragon's lair grown deeper,
    a stormwatch tower; the dragon's lair (already built) at its heart.
  - **Midnight, the haunted sea**: Morwen's tower grown, a drowned village half under the water, a ghost ship to
    board, more of its creatures.
- **The regatta — agreed**: a race round a course of buoys and islands against the other sloops on a set day each week,
  people on the quays watching; and races you start yourself against whoever you're playing with (see *Races*).
- **The seas a little larger — agreed**: more room to sail and race, and a world that feels bigger. About a fifth more
  (`K` in js/sloop.js, 600 → ~720 tiles a chart unit: an Emerald neighbour ~2–2.5 minutes away instead of 1.5–2).
- **Every sea's people and ships look their own — agreed**: what people wear, and the boats' hulls, sails and colours,
  differ from sea to sea.
- **Maybe**: tides (a causeway at low tide), ferry work.
- **Out**: diving under the spore sea, the dragon egg, two flags.

## Hoarfell — agreed: bigger and bougier, a grand hotel, hut-to-hut touring, the hidden valley
- **Fellhaven busier, bigger, bougier** (St. Moritz, Zermatt, Aspen): fondue, an ice rink, a heated outdoor pool
  steaming in the snow, sleigh rides, après-ski on a terrace, people in furs, the views.
- **The grand hotel — agreed**: a château, "a little bit Monaco-y", with a town beside it — the ski slopes, bars, fine
  restaurants, its own salon of casino games (Royale Link's, in its own fancier style). Proposed: Fellhaven stays the
  small working village; the château and its resort town are a second, bigger place up the line.
- **The mountain railway — agreed**, as a cutscene journey between towns or up to the hotel (panoramic carriage): really
  pretty, not too long but a little while — gorges, viaducts, glaciers, the peaks — and different by season and time of
  day.
- **Hut-to-hut ski touring — agreed**: several days across glaciers and passes, a hut each night; the way into the
  hidden valley.
- **The hidden valley — agreed, with enemies in tiers** (not the yetis; the tiers below agreed): warm from below (hot springs, moss,
  glacier caves, a cliff monastery with prayer flags); ice wolves on the outer slopes, rime beetles and ice bats in the
  glacier caves, rime golems and frost wraiths in the frozen monastery, and a glacier wyrm that swims under the snow
  (ripples warn you) at the heart. The yetis stay shy and friendly.
- **Out**: guiding jobs. **Maybe**: the dog sled.
